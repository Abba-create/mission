Skills and hope, taught wherever people are ready to learn.

Global Outreach Mission brings practical technical education, career guidance and teacher training to students, colleges and church communities in India and across the world.

Join or partner See our programs Join as a partner

What we teach

Three programs, one purpose: give people useful skills and the confidence to use them. Paid programs help fund the free ones.

Faculty development

For college teachers and trainers

Paid workshops
  • Outcome-based teaching and assessment
  • Running effective online and hybrid classes
  • Lab design and student project mentoring
  • Research and academic mentoring basics

VLSI and embedded systems

For engineering students and early-career professionals

Paid cohorts
  • Digital design fundamentals
  • Verilog and simulation
  • Microcontroller or FPGA projects
  • A capstone project you can show to employers

Digital skills and career guidance

For young people in rural areas, churches and children's homes

Free or subsidized
  • Computer basics and online safety
  • Communication skills, resumes and interviews
  • Choosing a career path and the next step after school
  • Character and values formation

Where we serve

We begin in India and build partnerships with churches, colleges and Christian organizations abroad. Sessions run online, so learners can join from anywhere.

India

Colleges, church communities and rural youth groups.

Africa

English-language training with church and school partners in Kenya, Uganda, Nigeria and Ghana.

Southeast Asia

Technical and career programs with partners in the Philippines and Malaysia.

Worldwide supporters

Churches and individuals in the USA, UK, Canada and Australia who sponsor learners.

Built by a teacher

Global Outreach Mission was started by Karunakar Endluri, an electronics engineer and educator who has spent years teaching engineering students and mentoring colleges through national programs.

The mission grew from a simple belief: good teaching should reach people no matter where they live or what they can afford.

Partner with us

Churches, colleges, NGOs and individual supporters can work with us in simple, low-risk steps.

  1. Start a conversation

    Tell us about your community and what your learners need.

  2. Try a pilot session

    We run a free introductory workshop online for your group.

  3. Grow together

    Host a full program, sponsor seats for learners, or invite us back each term.
